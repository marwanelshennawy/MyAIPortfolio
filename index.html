Embedded systems

Bluetooth-controlled robot car

A four-wheel Arduino car driven over Bluetooth with text commands from a phone. It stops by itself when a sensor detects an obstacle in its direction of travel.

  • Forward, backward, left, right, and four diagonal moves, with diagonals made by slowing one side's motors to half speed.
  • Five speed levels, from 20% to 100%.
  • Front and back obstacle sensors that stop the car before it hits something.
